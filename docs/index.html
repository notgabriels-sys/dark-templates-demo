Meridian

Meridian turns warehouse data into forecasts your buyers can act on — with the uncertainty attached, not hidden behind a single number.

What it does

Point Meridian at your ERP and point-of-sale systems. It reads them on a schedule, builds a demand forecast per SKU per location, and gives every forecast a range rather than a single guess. Buyers see how wrong the number might be before they commit cash to stock.

  • Read-only by default. Meridian never writes to your systems of record.
  • Ranges, not point estimates. Every forecast carries P10, P50 and P90.
  • Proposals, not automation. Draft purchase orders wait for a human.

Who it is for

Mid-market retailers and distributors — roughly 20 to 500 locations — who have outgrown spreadsheets but do not want an eighteen-month ERP replacement.

Before you start

You will need read credentials for at least one source system and an API key. The full pack covers both.
