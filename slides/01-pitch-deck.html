Northwind

Warehouse forecasting for mid-market retailers who still run on spreadsheets.

Confidential01
The problem

Retailers over-order by 20% because forecasting lives in spreadsheets.

  • Manual and stale. A planner rebuilds the same workbook every Monday from four systems.
  • No error bars. One number per SKU, no sense of how wrong it might be.
  • Cash sits on shelves. The average mid-market retailer carries 11 weeks of dead stock.
Northwind02
What we built

Forecasts that update themselves, with the uncertainty attached.

Connect

Read-only sync

Plugs into the ERP and POS in an afternoon. No migration, no rip-and-replace.

Forecast

Range, not a guess

Every SKU gets a P10–P90 range, so buyers can see the risk before committing cash.

Act

Order proposals

Draft purchase orders land in the buyer's queue. They approve or edit — nothing is automatic.
